People first in the age of AI

AI is changing how teams work faster than most people can absorb. I have never felt more empowered by a technology, and I take seriously that plenty of people feel the opposite. Leading people through it means holding three things in balance.

Pace

The change may simply be happening too fast. People deserve time to learn, to grieve a little for how the work used to be, and to adjust.

Agency

Most of us aren’t wired to break the system we work in, and those who are often aren’t empowered to. A faster pace needs real room to reshape the work.

Self

We gravitate toward what we’re good at, and it becomes part of who we are. When the work changes, identity and self-worth change with it, and they deserve care.

You can’t solve one without solving all three. So I lean on candid, authentic communication about what is changing and where to learn and grow, shaped for each person. Pure optimism or pure pessimism will both land flat.
